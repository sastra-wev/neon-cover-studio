# Neon Cover Studio

A static, fully offline web app for composing GTA-style cover art: a collage of framed photo panels with bold display lettering, built for the make.fun bounty [gta6-cover-art-01](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-cover-art-01).

Drop in your own photos (file picker or drag & drop, as many as you like), give each panel its own photo — or stretch one photo across every panel — and arrange the shot per panel by dragging on the canvas, zooming with the slider or mouse wheel, and switching between fill and fit. Pick from four poster layouts (classic 5-panel collage, 3 wide panels, 2-panel split, full hero) separated by white gutters, grade the colors in-canvas (Sunset Vice, Neon Night, Duotone, B&W Comic, or none), edit the big Anton display title and caption with color, size and position controls, and export a finished PNG at 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080.

Everything runs locally in the browser with vanilla JS modules — no uploads, no network requests, your photos never leave the page. The Anton typeface is vendored locally under the SIL Open Font License (see `app/fonts/OFL.txt`). All artwork and design are original; no Rockstar Games logos, wordmarks, art or fonts are used.
